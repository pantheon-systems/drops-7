Drupal 7.108, 2026-11-04
------------------------
- Security: Restores the `DrupalCacheArray::__destruct()` hardening from SA-CORE-2024-008 that was lost in Drupal 7.103.
- Security: Addresses an access bypass caused by case insensitive matching of external authentication names.

Drupal 7.107, 2026-10-14
------------------------
- Backport SA-CORE-2026-014. Site owners should review whether any custom code uses `#region_callback`, and test to ensure the Field UI tables continue to work as expected.

Drupal 7.106, 2026-08-19
------------------------
- Security: Addresses information disclosure of image style derivatives stored on file schemes other than the core private scheme.

Drupal 7.105, 2025-10-01
------------------------
- PHP 8.4 support
  - To reduce noise in logs, consider adding
    ini_set('error_reporting', E_ALL & ~E_DEPRECATED & ~E_USER_DEPRECATED);
    to settings.php or otherwise disable deprecation error reporting.
